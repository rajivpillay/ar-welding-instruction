Unit objective

Using the provided AR System Evaluation, evaluate an AR welding training system for fit with your existing welding theory and lab practices, completing every section.

Your role

You’re the subject matter expert in your lab. AR is the new piece. Unit 1 was about getting familiar with what an AR system does and how it might help your program. You’re the one who will work with it alongside your students, so you’re the right person to evaluate it.

What to carry forward

  • An AR session gives you two things to work with: what the student tells you, and what the system records. Start with the student.
  • AR supplements live welding. It builds the basics without the hazards. Heat, spatter, and real equipment still come from the booth.
  • A vendor’s claim is a reason to ask questions, not a reason to buy.
  • Set two goals before you look at any system: one for your program, and one for your students.
  • The AR System Evaluation turns your goals and what you learn about a system into a recommendation. “Not sure” becomes a question for the vendor.

Coming up in Unit 2

Unit 1 showed you what a session produces. Unit 2 teaches you to read it: what each part of technique measures, what counts as out of range for a given joint and position, and how to turn a reading into a cue a student can act on at the machine.
