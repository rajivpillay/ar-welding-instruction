Glossary

AR and simulation terms you’ll meet in Units 1 and 2 and in vendor materials.

Definitions are written for welding instructors new to AR. Each term is tagged:

  • General term: used across AR and simulation, not tied to one product.
  • Miller term: Miller AugmentedArc vocabulary. Other systems may use a different name for the same thing.
  • Miller usage: a general term that Miller uses in its own way.

Welding terms you already know, such as CTWD, travel angle, and 2F, are not included.

Attempt Miller term
One try at a task, saved with its date and time and marked pass (green check) or fail (red X).
Augmented reality (AR) General term
Digital images placed over a live view of the real surroundings. In welding simulation, a virtual arc and weld are shown on a real practice piece seen through the helmet.
Equipment settings (scored) Miller term
Whether the student set the machine within range, such as voltage and wire feed speed. Checked separately from technique.
Exercise Miller term
One simulated welding task, set up with a joint type, position, material thickness, process, filler, and gas.
Exercise settings Miller term
Options chosen before an exercise: difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced); equipment-setting guides on or off; equipment values preset or kept from the last session; real-time parameter guides on or off.
Gun angles Miller usage
The simulator’s collective term for the angles of the gun. The analysis screen reports them separately as work angle and travel angle.
Muscle memory General term
Everyday term for a motor skill that becomes automatic through repetition. Vendor materials use it to claim simulator practice carries over to live welding; how much carries over is what the research is still testing.
Overall score Miller term
The single score for an attempt, shown with the replay. On the helmet analysis screen it appears as the technique score against a target.
Post-weld feedback General term
Feedback shown after the weld is finished, such as scores and replays, used to review a completed attempt.
Real-time feedback General term
Feedback the trainee sees while welding, so technique can be corrected mid-pass.
Real-time parameter guides Miller term
On-screen markers shown during the weld for contact-tip-to-work distance, gun angles, travel speed, and aim. A guide shows red until technique is corrected, then green. Also called “guides.”
Replay Miller term
Playback of the completed simulated weld, opened from the analysis screen.
Simulated helmet shade Miller term
How dark the helmet display goes during the simulated weld, adjustable from 8 to 13 to imitate a welding lens.
Simulation analysis screen Miller term
The screen shown after a simulated weld. It scores each parameter; parameters highlighted in red did not pass.
Target and range (min, max) Miller term
The value an exercise aims for on each parameter, and the lowest and highest values it accepts. Shown on the exercise setup screen before the weld.
Teacher Software Miller term
The instructor’s program for AugmentedArc: organizes courses, reviews student attempts, monitors the classroom live, and produces reports.
Technique parameters Miller term
The five things the simulator measures and scores during a weld: work angle, travel angle, CTWD, travel speed, and aim.
Technique score Miller term
The overall score for how the student welded, shown against a target score (for example 95 against 90). Separate from the equipment-settings check.
Workpiece Miller usage
The physical practice piece shaped like a joint (T, butt, lap, V-groove) that the helmet is aimed at. The simulated weld is laid on it.
