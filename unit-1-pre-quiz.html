Unit objective

Using the provided AR System Evaluation, evaluate an AR welding training system for fit with your existing welding theory and lab practices, completing every section.

Five questions before the lessons begin. This isn’t graded. It shows what you already know, so you can see what you learn. You’ll see your score, but not the answers. Questions like these come back in the Knowledge Check.

1. After an AR session, which of these can only the student tell you?
2. A student has scored well on the simulator for weeks. What do they still need the live booth for?
3. A brochure says a simulator “cuts training hours by 65%.” What’s the best first question to ask?
4. Which of these is an instructional goal, not an operational one?
5. You’re evaluating a system from vendor materials, and nothing says whether it supports overhead welding. What do you mark?
