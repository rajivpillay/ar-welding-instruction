What this page is

This is a pipeline test, not the course shell. It exists to confirm three things before the real build starts:

  1. Files in site/ deploy to GitHub Pages and are reachable at a public URL.
  2. The accessibility baseline works: skip link, heading order, visible focus, keyboard navigation.
  3. Light and dark rendering both hold up, and the layout works at phone width.

Check these yourself

  • Press Tab from the top of the page. The first stop should be a “Skip to main content” link that becomes visible.
  • Keep tabbing. Every focused element should show a clear outline.
  • Open this on your phone. Nothing should scroll sideways.
  • Switch your system appearance between light and dark. Both should stay readable.
