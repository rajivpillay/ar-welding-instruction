Professional Development · Self-Paced · Four Units

Integrating Augmented Reality into Welding Instruction

A course for secondary Career and Technical Education welding instructors preparing to bring augmented reality simulation into an existing welding program.

What this course prepares you to do

Draft objectives — finalized in Week 2

  • Evaluate AR welding tools and their features.
  • Interpret AR simulator feedback.
  • Align AR use with established welding objectives and safety requirements.
  • Develop an AR-to-live-welding integration plan.

The four units

Take them in order. Each one builds toward your integration plan.

No prior AR experience needed. About an hour per unit. Bring your current welding curriculum for Units 3 and 4.
