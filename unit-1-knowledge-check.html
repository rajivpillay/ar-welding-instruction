Unit objective

Using the provided AR System Evaluation, evaluate an AR welding training system for fit with your existing welding theory and lab practices, completing every section.

Ten questions. The first five are like the Pre-Quiz, so you can see what changed. The other five use different formats. If you get 8 or more right, you’re ready for the Unit Task. If not, the results will point you to the topics to review, and you can try again or continue to the task.

1. After a session, which of these does the system record on its own?

2. A student has scored well on the simulator for weeks. What do they still need the live booth for?

3. A brochure says a simulator gives “60% less material waste.” What’s the best first question?

4. Which of these is an operational goal, not an instructional one?

5. You’re evaluating a system from vendor materials, and nothing says whether it supports overhead welding. What do you mark?

6. Your department chair says: “If we buy a simulator, students won’t need as much booth time before they’re job-ready.” What’s the best response?

7. A student finishes their first simulator session with a low score and looks frustrated. What do you do first?

8. On this screen, which setting turns off the red and green guides students see while they weld?

9. Which two of these goals could you check at the end of a term? Choose two.

10. These come from Miller materials. Can each one be checked, or is it a slogan? All four must be right to earn the point.

“Builds Efficiency and Accuracy”

“65% fewer training hours”

“Transforming Tomorrow’s Welders with Today’s Technology”
