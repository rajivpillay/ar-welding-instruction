Unit objective

Given a sample AR simulator performance report, interpret the feedback data and provide constructive input to the welding student, identifying the indicator outside the acceptable range and recommending one specific technique modification.

Ten questions. The first five are like the Pre-Quiz, so you can see what changed. The other five use different formats. If you get 8 or more right, you’re ready for the Unit Task. If not, the results will point you to the topics to review, and you can try again or continue to the task.
