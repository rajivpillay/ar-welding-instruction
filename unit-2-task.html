Unit objective

Given a sample AR simulator performance report, interpret the feedback data and provide constructive input to the welding student, identifying the indicator outside the acceptable range and recommending one specific technique modification.

What you’ll do

Read a sample simulator report. Name the indicator that is outside the acceptable range, and write the one technique change you would recommend to the student.

Worked example: Unit 2 Task

1. Which indicator is outside the acceptable range?

Work angle. Its line drops below the 30° minimum on the setup screen. Every other parameter stays in range.

Rubric: The indicator, 2. It names the one indicator outside the range and judges it against the setup screen, not the graph.

2. What does the reading show?

The average, 38°, is inside the 30–60° range, but the line drops below 30° through the middle third of the weld and then recovers. The student sets the angle correctly at the start and loses it partway through.

Rubric: The reading, 2. It says what the line does and where along the weld. “Work angle averaged 38° and scored 81” would score 1: it gives only the average and the score.

3. What one technique change would you recommend?

“Your work angle is dropping in the middle of the weld. Set the gun at 45 degrees, then do a dry run along the whole joint and watch that the angle holds as you pass the middle. On the next weld, check your wrist when you reach the halfway point.”

Rubric: The cue, 2. It names one change (work angle), says what to do rather than reading the number aloud, and gives the student two ways to check themselves: the dry run and the halfway check.
