Unit objective

Given a set of CTE Welding and Materials Joining pathway standards, analyze which can be supported by AR simulator practice and which require live instruction, completing every row of the alignment table.

Five questions before the lessons begin. This isn’t graded. It shows what you already know, so you can see what you learn. You’ll see your score, but not the answers. Questions like these come back in the Knowledge Check.

1. C2.3 ends with “a finished product that meets the standards of the AWS or a similar industry standard.” What does that part of the standard tell you?
2. Which of these can a student practice on a typical AR welding simulator?
3. A student passes every simulator exercise for a process. What does that show?
4. A colleague marks every row of their Alignment Table “partially supported.” What’s the problem?
5. Which safety habit can simulator practice build?
