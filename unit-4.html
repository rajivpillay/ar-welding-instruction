Unit objective

Using your completed alignment table, develop an AR-to-live-welding integration plan for one course in one upcoming term, completing every section of the plan template.

What you will be able to do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

  1. Plan regular, scheduled simulator use for each learner, with a progress log for each student.
  2. Plan the demonstrations and the live instruction your Alignment Table identified, including safety.
  3. Set a readiness criterion for moving from simulator to booth, and plan the re-orientation phase.

What this unit covers

A simulator works in a welding program when students use it consistently and move to live welding at the right time. This unit turns your Alignment Table into a plan for one course in one term: when each student practices on the simulator, how you track their progress, which demonstrations and live instruction you give, when a student is ready for the booth, and how you support them through their first live sessions.

Estimated time: about 90 minutes, not counting breaks. Your work saves in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

Before you start: complete the Unit 3 Alignment Table. Your plan starts from it.

The six parts

  1. Overview, what the unit covers and what you will be able to do.
  2. Pre-Quiz, a short check of what you already know, before the content.
  3. Knowledge Building, the instruction itself.
  4. Knowledge Check, ten questions with feedback.
  5. Unit Task, where you build your Integration Plan.
  6. Summary, what to take into your term.
