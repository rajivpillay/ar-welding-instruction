Unit objective

Given a set of CTE Welding and Materials Joining pathway standards, analyze which can be supported by AR simulator practice and which require live instruction, completing every row of the alignment table.

Ten questions. The first five are like the Pre-Quiz, so you can see what changed. The other five use different formats. If you get 8 or more right, you’re ready for the Unit Task. If not, the results will point you to the topics to review, and you can try again or continue to the task.

1. C3.1 ends with “a finished part or product that meets the standards of the AWS or similar industry welding standards.” What does that phrase tell you?

2. Which of these can a student practice on a typical AR welding simulator?

3. A student’s simulator scores are above target in all four positions. For C2.3, what do the scores show?

4. A row in an Alignment Table reads: “C2.1. Partially supported.” What does it still need?

5. Which safety habit can simulator practice build?

6. Rate each standard. All three must be right to earn the point.

C6.1 Select and use appropriate welding tools, equipment, and inspection devices to manufacture parts or products.

C4.2 Apply the processes used for finishing welded materials.

C5.4 Analyze and identify the steps to check for distortion, joint misalignment, and poor fit-up before and after welding.

7. Which three of these hazards does Anchor Standard 6.4 name? Choose three.

8. A student finishes a bead and right away sets the electrode holder down on the steel table, electrode still in it. What’s the concern?

9. In the first week of live welding, a student who did well on the simulator welds worse than expected. What does this unit say to expect?

10. On the simulator, or live only? All four must be right to earn the point.
