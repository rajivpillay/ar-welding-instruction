Unit objective

Using your completed alignment table, develop an AR-to-live-welding integration plan for one course in one upcoming term, completing every section of the plan template.

Five questions before the lessons begin. This isn’t graded. It shows what you already know, so you can see what you learn. You’ll see your score, but not the answers. Questions like these come back in the Knowledge Check.

1. What matters most for simulator practice to work across a term?
2. When should an instructor give a basic demonstration during simulator practice?
3. Which is the best sign a student may be ready to move from the simulator to the booth?
4. How many live sessions does it typically take a student to get used to heat and smoke after simulator practice?
5. Where does the must-teach-live list in your Integration Plan come from?
