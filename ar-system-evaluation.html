Your goals

Part 1: Checklist

18 quick questions in eight areas. “Not sure” is an honest answer: add it to your list of questions for the vendor.

1. Training features

Can you set the exercise: joint, position, process, and filler?

Can you adjust the difficulty?

Can you turn the guides on and off?

2. Ease of use

Could an instructor new to the system set up a session without the vendor there?

Could a beginning student start an exercise on their own?

3. Feedback and reports

Does it score each part of technique: angles, distance, speed, and aim?

Does it show the acceptable range for each exercise?

Does it save a record of every attempt?

4. Reading the feedback

Is it clear which part of technique needs work?

Could you turn a report into one cue for a student?

5. Processes and positions

Does it cover the processes you teach?

Does it cover the positions you teach?

6. Carry-over to live welding

Does the vendor offer evidence that skills carry over to live welding?

Does that evidence come from a study, rather than testimonials?

7. Cost

Do you know the full cost: system, software, updates, and replacement parts?

Would savings in steel offset the cost, and do you know over how long?

8. Technical support

Does the vendor provide training for instructors?

Do you know who to call when it breaks, and how fast they respond?
