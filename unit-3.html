Unit objective

Given a set of CTE Welding and Materials Joining pathway standards, analyze which can be supported by AR simulator practice and which require live instruction, completing every row of the alignment table.

What you will be able to do

By the end of this unit you will be able to:

  1. Identify what a standard asks a student to do and produce, including the industry standard the finished work must meet.
  2. Judge whether AR simulator practice supports a standard fully, partly, or not at all, based on what the practice actually exercises, and name the part it supports.
  3. Identify the safety requirements tied to a standard that must be taught at the live arc.

What this unit covers

Your welding standards say what students must be able to do and produce. This unit is about reading them closely: which parts simulator practice can carry, which need live instruction, and which safety requirements come with the live work. You finish with a completed Alignment Table, the starting point for your Unit 4 plan.

Estimated time: about 75 minutes, not counting breaks. Your work saves in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

The six parts

  1. Overview, what the unit covers and what you will be able to do.
  2. Pre-Quiz, a short check of what you already know, before the content.
  3. Knowledge Building, the instruction itself.
  4. Knowledge Check, ten questions with feedback.
  5. Unit Task, where you complete the Alignment Table and show you can meet the unit objective.
  6. Summary, what to carry into Unit 4.
